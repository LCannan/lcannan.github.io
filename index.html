RLT for Sub-Millimeter Pick-and-Place: Two-Stage Training and RL Handover

XtalPi, Shanghai · Physical Intelligence · Embodied AI Algorithm Intern | Jul–Oct 2026

Problem
Built a staged manipulation system on a Flexiv arm to improve placement with sub-millimeter insertion clearance. A π0.5-based VLA policy handles grasping and transport, while RL takes over precision placement, connecting policy training, stage recognition and real-robot execution.
Method
Fine-tuned π0.5 with SFT, then froze the backbone and trained an encoder/decoder to support online residual RL with TD3. Developed a temporal classifier using ResNet and 3D convolutions to guide RL handover. TCP image streaming and cross-host client–server rollouts support human intervention, success/failure labels and replay collection from RL-controlled segments.
Result
After 20 minutes of online RL rollouts, achieved a 92% precision-placement success rate across 50 real-robot trials, up from the π0.5 baseline of 40% (+52 percentage points). Stage-classification accuracy exceeded 99%.

HIL-SERL: Learning Real-Robot Insertion on a Flexiv Arm

XtalPi, Shanghai · Physical Intelligence · Embodied AI Algorithm Intern | Jul–Oct 2026

Problem
Integrated robot control, visual observations and online policy learning in a HIL-SERL system for autonomous insertion on the Flexiv Rizon 4s. Human demonstrations and real-time interventions support policy training, deployment and evaluation on the robot.
Method
Connected Flexiv RDK control interfaces to the learning system through a Flask HTTP service, with four RealSense views and end-effector pose, velocity, force and torque observations. Command-pose integration and velocity feedforward reduce stop–start motion and target lag. The x/y/z/yaw action space includes workspace limits, predictive joint-limit checks and insertion-force limits.
Result
Achieved autonomous insertion after approximately 50 minutes of training. In 20 real-robot trials without human intervention, successfully inserted the grasped object into the target 18 times (90%), with initial x/y offsets randomized within ±5 cm and angular offsets within ±5°.

PriorVLA Policy Optimization and Heuristic DAgger for Robot Manipulation

Research Project | Jan–Aug 2026

Problem
Reproduced and improved PriorVLA on OpenPI with a π0.5 backbone, validated it in ManiSkill and deployed it on a Flexiv arm. The system connects multi-view RealSense observations, teleoperation, training with corrective data and policy execution.
Method
Built a multi-GPU fine-tuning pipeline with observation gating and eight learnable state queries. Investigated timestamp misalignment between observations and action labels and tuned the action-chunk length. Used heuristic DAgger interventions to address recurring task failures, collecting 20 corrective trajectories for training the improved policy.
Result
Using 30.6% of the trainable parameters in the full-fine-tuning baseline, improved ManiSkill PegInsertion success from 43.03% to 51.37%. Training with corrective trajectories raised real-robot task success from 40% for the π0.5 baseline to 76%.
