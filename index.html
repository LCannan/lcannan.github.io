RLT Two-Stage Training & Critical-Phase RL for Sub-Millimeter Pick-and-Place

XtalPi · Physical Intelligence · Algorithm Intern | Jul–Sep 2026

Problem
For sub-millimeter-clearance pick-and-place on a Flexiv arm, built a π0.5-based staged system: VLA handles grasping and transport, while RL takes over precision placement.
Method
Built a two-stage pipeline: SFT for π0.5, followed by a frozen backbone with Encoder/Decoder training and online TD3. A ResNet + 3D-convolution classifier identifies five manipulation stages. RealSense TCP streaming and cross-host client–server rollouts support stage switching, human intervention and replay collection.
Result
After 70 minutes of online RL rollouts, precision-placement success improved from 40% to 92% across 50 real-robot trials; stage-classification accuracy exceeded 99%.
Technical details
  • The classifier covers searching, grasping, transporting, inserting and success to determine when RL should take over.
  • Developed a TCP image relay to let multiple hosts subscribe to a RealSense camera; only RL-controlled segments enter the replay buffer.
Human-in-the-Loop RL · HIL-SERL · Real-World Insertion

HIL-SERL Real-Robot Reinforcement Learning for Insertion on a Flexiv Arm

XtalPi · Physical Intelligence · Algorithm Intern | Jul–Sep 2026

Problem
Built a human-in-the-loop RL system for the Flexiv Rizon 4s, combining demonstrations and real-time intervention to train and deploy policies for real-world insertion.
Method
Integrated Flexiv RDK control and feedback through a Flask HTTP bridge, using four RealSense views plus end-effector pose, velocity, force and torque. Command-pose integration and velocity feedforward improve motion smoothness; a four-dimensional x/y/z/yaw action space includes workspace, joint-limit and insertion-force constraints.
Result
Achieved autonomous insertion after approximately 50 minutes. All 20 real-robot evaluations succeeded without human intervention, with initial x/y offsets of ±5 cm and angular offsets of ±5°.
Technical details
  • Connected hardware control, visual observations, online policy learning and real-robot interaction in one pipeline.
  • Addressed periodic stop–start behavior and target lag in incremental control, with motion-control adaptations and contact-force limits.
VLA · Parameter-Efficient Training · Sim & Real

PriorVLA Policy Optimization & Real-Robot Deployment

Research Project · Core Contributor | Jan–Jun 2026

Problem
Reproduced and improved PriorVLA on OpenPI, with simulation validation and Flexiv deployment, connecting multi-view RealSense observations, teleoperation, heuristic DAgger, training and inference.
Method
Built a multi-GPU π0.5 fine-tuning pipeline with observation gating and eight learnable state queries. Deployed synchronous/asynchronous inference, diagnosed observation–action timestamp misalignment and tuned action-chunk length. Progress-stagnation-triggered DAgger added 20 corrective trajectories.
Result
Using 30.6% of the full-fine-tuning baseline’s trainable parameters, improved ManiSkill PegInsertion success from 43.03% to 51.37%. With corrective-trajectory training, real-robot precision pick-and-place success increased from the π0.5 baseline of 40% to 76%.
